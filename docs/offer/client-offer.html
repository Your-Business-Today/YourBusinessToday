Case study

Three businesses that run on a portal we built.

A construction company, a fire door company and a property management company, all in the past few months. Each portal was built in weeks around how that business works, and each belongs to the business that runs it.

A construction company

The building was never the problem. It was everything around it. The day on site arrived in the office as messages and photographs that somebody had to turn into a report for the client. Drawings came back marked up. Valuations were put together by hand. The idea that fixed it fits in one sentence: everything that reaches the business is a communication, a person makes one decision about it, and the portal groups it and writes what has to be written.

Every project
at the stage it is really at, with the decisions that need the owner beside it.
13 weeks
of cash ahead, read from the bills and invoices in Xero, lowest week flagged.
One decision
per email, photograph or drawing that arrives. The portal files it and writes it up.
Their own AI
reads a job or raises a request from the assistant the team already use.

A fire door company

A fire door company lives on proof. Every doorset has to be surveyed, every inspection recorded and every certificate to hand the day the building owner asks for it. The quote, the job and the invoice have to agree without anyone typing the same thing three times.

Every doorset
on its own record, from the survey through each inspection to the certificate.
Quote to job
a survey becomes a quote and an accepted quote becomes the job, in step with Xero.
The handover pack
put together from the job’s own records, with the certificates in it.
Their own AI
reads a job, raises a to-do or files an email from the assistant the team already use.

A property management company

A property management company is judged on response. A fault is reported, a visit is booked, the work is done and proved, and the certificate, the invoice and the key all have to be where they should be, whoever was on the road that day.

Every property
with its tenants, its certificates, its keys and the jobs on it in one place.
Every visit
booked, done and recorded by the team on the road, photographs and receipts included.
Invoiced from the work
work orders, valuations and variations become the invoice in Xero without being retyped.
Their own AI
answers what needs them today and raises the to-do, from the assistant the team already use.
