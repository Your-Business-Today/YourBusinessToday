The process

Five steps. You can stop after any of them.

  1. Step 1

    Map your data sources

    Two weeks · fixed price · no commitment to go further

    Every business creates its data somewhere. The sheet a quote is worked out in. The document a report is typed into. The message a job is agreed on. The photograph that proves the work was done. We spend two weeks with the people who make them and we map every source.

    You get: a written map of your data sources. What each one creates and who creates it and where it goes next. With it the order the harness should take them in and a fixed price for building it. Yours to keep whether or not you go further.

  2. Step 2

    Build the harness

    The first build stage · six to eight weeks · agreed before it starts

    The harness is one secure place that holds what those sources hold. Every fact about your business has its place in it and every value is checked before it goes in. Your people keep working the way they work. The harness is where the result now lives.

    You get: your data in one place you can see and search with the first sources retired into it. From this stage on you have dashboards over the harness and your AI assistant connected to it. Both are live from the first week.

  3. Step 3

    Teach the assistant the processes

    One process a stage · agreed before it starts · stop after any stage

    Around the harness sit the processes. The valuation put together every month. The report written from the week on site. The chase for the invoice that is late. We write each one as a skill your own AI assistant runs from start to finish. It reads the harness for what it needs and writes the result back.

    You get: one process running itself. The judgement stays with the person who has it and the repetitive work goes to the assistant. Every output lands in the harness so the next process can start from it.

  1. Step 4

    Two ways in

    Included with the harness · grows with every skill

    Everything in the harness is reached in two ways. Dashboards show it the way any software would with a screen for each thing and a view for each question. Your AI assistant does exactly what the website does from inside a chat. The same actions and the same checks with no browser in the middle.

    You get: one set of roles deciding who may see and do what. It is enforced on both in the same way. A person who cannot approve an invoice on the screen cannot approve it through their assistant either.

  2. Step 5

    Run it

    One monthly fee · cancel on a month’s notice

    Once a stage is paid for the harness and the skills are yours. So is the written record of how your business works. Most businesses ask us to stay because the work never stops changing. A new process to teach. A new record the business now needs.

    You get: everything kept running and kept current. Hosting and backups and support all sit inside one monthly figure. Your project in our project management system stays open so you can raise requests and watch them being built. A standing day of change work each month.

Case study

Jewel Bespoke Build

A family run construction company in Surrey. Jewel build loft conversions and extensions across the South of England and take on whole house renovations. The family has sixty five years of trade experience.

The building was never the problem. It was everything around it. The day on site arrived in the office as messages and photographs. Somebody then had to turn them into a report for the client. Drawings came back marked up. Valuations were put together by hand. The person who knew where everything was could never take a Friday off.

We spent a week on paper with the people who do the work. Then we spent a week proving the idea in code. Everything arriving at the business is a communication and a person makes one decision about it. After that the harness groups them and the assistant writes what has to be written.

Jewel now run on a portal that shows every project at the stage it is really at. A plain English request becomes a build they can watch. Their own AI assistant can read a job or raise a request without opening the site.
