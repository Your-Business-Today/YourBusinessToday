How you get your system back.

Your data and your system are yours, whatever happens to us.

  1. 01

    Your data is yours

    You own everything you put in. We never hold it back, not even over an unpaid bill.

  2. 02

    Export it yourself, any time

    Your administrator presses Export and gets the whole database, a spreadsheet file per table, the layout of the data and every stored file. No asking us, no charge, as often as you like.

  3. 03

    A copy in storage you own, every month

    Every month we write a copy of your database, the source code and the infrastructure definitions to storage you own and control. Open one now and then to check it.

  4. 04

    Your system runs on its own

    It runs in its own Microsoft Azure subscription, kept for you alone. That subscription can be handed over to you whole.

  5. 05

    If you leave

    You get a final export, the source code and everything needed to run the system elsewhere, for every stage you have paid for. Then we delete your data and confirm it in writing.

  6. 06

    If we cannot continue

    If we stop trading, become insolvent or go silent on an urgent problem for five working days, you take over. We transfer the subscription and hand over the keys. You already hold the licence to do it.

The full terms are in Schedule 4: Exit and Continuity.

This page is a summary. If the two ever differ, Schedule 4 to our Master Services Agreement is what we sign and what we keep to.
