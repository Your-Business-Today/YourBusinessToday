## Background

YBT learns how a business runs and then builds and runs the software that runs it. This agreement is signed once. Each piece of work is then ordered separately on an Order Form that incorporates this agreement. Nothing is built, and nothing is owed, until an Order Form is signed.

## 1. How this agreement is put together

1.1 The contract between us is made up of this agreement, its schedules and each signed Order Form. The schedules are:

- Schedule 1 — Data Processing Agreement (including the sub-processor register);
- Schedule 2 — Service Levels and Support;
- Schedule 3 — Acceptable Use Policy;
- Schedule 4 — Exit and Continuity.

  1.2 **Order of precedence.** If there is a conflict, it is resolved in this order: (a) Schedule 1 (Data Processing Agreement), so far as the conflict concerns personal data; (b) the clauses of this agreement; (c) the other schedules; (d) the Order Form. An Order Form overrides this agreement only where it names the clause it is changing and says that it is doing so.

  1.3 **No other terms.** No terms printed on, attached to or referred to in a purchase order, supplier portal, invoice or any other document of yours apply, even if we accept or act on that document. A purchase order is for your internal reference only. We will not start work against a purchase order without your written confirmation that this agreement governs it.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 In this agreement:

- "**Background IP**" means everything YBT owned or developed before or outside an Order Form — its frameworks, libraries, templates, tooling, methods and know-how — and any improvement to them that is not specific to you.
- "**Business Day**" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England.
- "**Client Data**" means all data, including personal data, that you or your users put into the Solution or that we receive from you.
- "**Deliverables**" means everything we produce for you under an Order Form: the source code, database schema, configuration, infrastructure definitions and documentation specific to you, and the written record of how your business works.
- "**Foreground IP**" means the intellectual property rights in the Deliverables, other than Background IP and third-party components.
- "**Order Form**" means a signed order for a Discovery stage, a Build stage or the Run service, in the form YBT provides.
- "**Run service**" means YBT hosting, operating, monitoring, backing up, updating and supporting the Solution for a monthly fee.
- "**Solution**" means the software YBT builds for you and, while you take the Run service, the managed environment in which it runs.

## 3. Ordering work in stages

3.1 Work is ordered in stages. Each Discovery or Build stage has its own Order Form setting out its scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria, fee and dates.

3.2 Signing an Order Form commits both of us to that stage only. You are never obliged to order the next stage, and choosing not to is how you stop. There is no termination fee for not ordering further stages.

3.3 Fees for a completed stage are not refundable. A stage is complete when its Deliverables are accepted under clause 4.

3.4 The Run service is ordered on its own Order Form and runs month to month until either of us ends it under clause 13.

## 4. Acceptance

4.1 When a Build stage is ready we will tell you in writing and make it available for you to test against the acceptance criteria in the Order Form.

4.2 You have ten Business Days to accept it or tell us in writing, with reasons, which acceptance criterion it fails. We will fix those failures and offer it again, and the same process repeats.

4.3 A stage is accepted when you say so in writing, when the ten Business Days pass without a written rejection, or when you start using it in live operation — whichever is first. A failure that is not against an acceptance criterion is not a reason to reject; we will deal with it as support under Schedule 2.

## 5. Changes

5.1 Scope, price or dates in a signed Order Form change only by a written change request signed by both of us, stating the effect on price and timetable.

5.2 We do not start changed work until the change request is signed. Spoken instructions, emails that are not a signed change request, and messages in the portal do not vary an Order Form.

## 6. Your responsibilities

6.1 You will give us timely access to the people, information and systems named in the Order Form, make decisions through one named person who can decide, and make sure that the data you give us is data you are entitled to share.

6.2 Where you are late in doing something we depend on, the dates that depend on it move by at least the same time, and we may charge for the time lost at the day rate in the Order Form.

6.3 You are responsible for your users' compliance with the Acceptable Use Policy in Schedule 3, and for keeping your own users' sign-in credentials secure.

## 7. Fees and payment

7.1 You will pay the fees in each Order Form. Unless the Order Form says otherwise, we invoice a Discovery stage on signature, a Build stage 50% on signature and 50% on acceptance, and the Run service monthly in advance.

7.2 Invoices are payable within 14 days of the invoice date. All fees are exclusive of VAT, which is added where it applies.

7.3 **Late payment.** If an invoice is not paid on time we may charge interest and compensation under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998, at 8% a year above the Bank of England base rate, from the due date until payment.

7.4 **Suspension.** If an undisputed invoice is more than 30 days overdue, we may give you written notice that we will suspend your users' access to the Solution unless it is paid within a further 14 days. If it is not, we may suspend access until it is. **Suspension never suspends our duties to export, return and delete Client Data under Schedule 1 and Schedule 4. We will never withhold your data to secure payment.**

7.5 **Run service price changes.** We may change the monthly Run service fee on at least 60 days' written notice. If you do not accept the change you may end the Run service on notice given before the change takes effect, without paying the new price.

## 8. The managed Solution

8.1 While you take the Run service, YBT provides the Solution as a single managed service. YBT contracts for all of the infrastructure and platform services underneath it (including Microsoft Azure) in its own name and at its own risk, and is your only counterparty for them. You acquire the Solution, not any underlying cloud service, and nothing in this agreement is a resale, sub-licence or redistribution of a third-party service.

8.2 The Solution runs in a Microsoft Azure subscription held by YBT and dedicated to you alone, in the Microsoft Azure regions the Order Form names, in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, separated from every other client's systems. The monthly fee is a single inclusive figure; we do not pass through or itemise the cost of underlying services.

8.3 Schedule 4 sets out how the Solution and its subscription can be transferred to you, or to someone you nominate, when the Run service ends or if YBT cannot continue.

## 9. Intellectual property

9.1 **Assignment of Foreground IP.** In consideration of the fees for each stage, YBT hereby assigns to the Client, with full title guarantee and by way of present assignment of present and future rights, all Foreground IP in the Deliverables of that stage, with effect from the date the fees for that stage are paid in full. Until then, you have a licence to use the Deliverables for the purpose of testing and accepting them.

9.2 **Moral rights.** YBT will procure that each individual who creates Deliverables waives their moral rights in them, so far as the law allows.

9.3 **Background IP.** YBT keeps its Background IP. It grants you a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to use, copy and modify the Background IP embedded in the Deliverables, for the purpose of using, maintaining and developing the Solution, including through another supplier. You may not sell the Background IP on its own.

9.4 **Our know-how.** Nothing in this agreement stops YBT reusing general skills, techniques, ideas and know-how that are not specific to you and do not contain your Confidential Information or Client Data.

9.5 **Open-source components.** The Order Form (or the documentation delivered with a stage) lists the material open-source components in the Deliverables and their licences. We will not include a component under a copyleft licence (such as the GPL or AGPL) in anything distributed to you without your written consent.

9.6 **AI-assisted development.** We use AI tools to help write code and documents, and every Deliverable is reviewed by a person before it is delivered. The assignment in clause 9.1 covers whatever rights subsist in AI-assisted output, and as between us, those rights belong to you on payment. We do not warrant that copyright subsists in any particular piece of AI-assisted output. Client Data is never used to train a third-party AI model, and we only send Client Data to an AI service named in the sub-processor register in Schedule 1.

9.7 **Warranty against infringement.** YBT warrants that, to its knowledge, the Deliverables (excluding open-source components and anything you supplied) do not infringe any third party's intellectual property rights.

## 10. Your data

10.1 You own the Client Data. You are the controller of any personal data in it and YBT is your processor. Schedule 1 governs how we process personal data.

10.2 You grant YBT a limited, non-exclusive, revocable licence to host, store, copy, transmit and process the Client Data solely to provide the services. The licence ends when this agreement ends, subject to Schedule 4.

10.3 YBT will not use Client Data for its own purposes — including analytics, benchmarking, product development, case studies or AI training — and will never combine it with another client's data.

10.4 Your authorised administrator can export the Client Data at any time, without asking us, in the formats named in Schedule 4.

## 11. Confidentiality

11.1 Each of us will keep the other's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the purposes of this agreement, and disclose it only to people who need it for those purposes and are bound by the same duty. "Confidential Information" means information disclosed by one of us to the other that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would regard as confidential, including Client Data and how your business works.

11.2 This does not apply to information that is or becomes public other than through a breach of this clause, that the recipient already lawfully had, or that must be disclosed by law or a regulator (with notice to the other where lawful).

11.3 We will not name you as a client or publish anything about the work without your written agreement.

11.4 This clause continues for five years after this agreement ends, and indefinitely for Client Data.

## 12. Liability

12.1 **Nothing limits** either party's liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be limited; or the Client's liability to pay fees properly due.

12.2 **Excluded loss.** Subject to clause 12.1, neither of us is liable for loss of profits, revenue, business, goodwill or anticipated savings, or for any indirect or consequential loss.

12.3 **Loss of data.** While YBT is paid to host and back up Client Data, YBT's liability for loss or corruption of Client Data is to restore it from the most recent backup it holds, at its own cost, and to pay the Client's reasonable costs of re-entering data lost since that backup, within the cap in clause 12.5. The Client will check restored data promptly and tell YBT of any problem.

12.4 **General cap.** Subject to clauses 12.1 and 12.5, each party's total liability arising under or in connection with this agreement in any contract year is limited to the greater of (a) the fees paid and payable by the Client in the 12 months before the event giving rise to the claim, and (b) for a claim arising from a Build stage, the fee for that stage.

12.5 **Data protection and confidentiality cap.** Subject to clause 12.1, each party's total liability for breach of clause 11, Schedule 1 or data protection law is limited to £[250,000] in aggregate in any contract year. This separate cap is set by reference to the insurance YBT carries under clause 14.

12.6 Each party will take reasonable steps to mitigate any loss it claims for. Service credits under Schedule 2 are the sole remedy for failing to meet an availability target, but not for any other breach.

12.7 The parties agree that these limits are reasonable, having regard to the fees, the insurance available to YBT, and the Client's ability to insure its own business.

## 13. Term and ending

13.1 This agreement starts on the date it is signed and continues until every Order Form under it has ended and either of us gives written notice to end it.

13.2 **Ending the Run service.** Either of us may end the Run service on one calendar month's written notice, expiring at the end of a billing month. We will not end the Run service on less than three months' notice unless clause 13.3 applies.

13.3 **Ending for cause.** Either of us may end this agreement or any Order Form immediately by written notice if the other (a) commits a material breach that cannot be remedied, or that is not remedied within 30 days of written notice describing it; or (b) becomes insolvent, enters administration or liquidation, or ceases to trade.

13.4 **What happens when it ends.** You pay for work done and services provided up to the end date. Schedule 4 (Exit and Continuity) applies. Clauses 7.3, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13.4, 15 and 16, and Schedules 1 and 4, survive.

## 14. Insurance

14.1 YBT will maintain, with a reputable insurer, for the term of this agreement and for six years afterwards:

- professional indemnity insurance on a technology wording with a limit of not less than £[2,000,000] for any one claim;
- cyber liability insurance with a limit of not less than £[250,000];
- public liability insurance with a limit of not less than £[2,000,000] for any one event; and
- employers' liability insurance as the law requires, from the time YBT employs anyone.

  14.2 YBT will give the Client a copy of its insurance certificates on request.

## 15. Data protection, subcontracting and people

15.1 Schedule 1 applies to all processing of personal data under this agreement.

15.2 YBT may use subcontractors, but remains responsible for their work as if it were its own. Sub-processors of personal data are governed by Schedule 1.

15.3 Neither of us will solicit for employment anyone employed by the other and involved in the services, during this agreement and for six months afterwards, other than through a general advertisement.

## 16. General

16.1 **Force majeure.** Neither of us is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond our reasonable control, provided we tell the other promptly and do what we reasonably can to limit the effect. This does not excuse the duties in Schedule 1 or Schedule 4 for longer than the event itself lasts.

16.2 **Notices** must be in writing and sent by email to the addresses in the signature block (or any other address notified in writing). A notice is received at 9:00 on the next Business Day after sending.

16.3 **Assignment.** Neither of us may transfer this agreement without the other's written consent, which is not to be unreasonably withheld, except that YBT may transfer it to a successor under Schedule 4.

16.4 **Entire agreement.** This agreement is the whole agreement between us about its subject and replaces anything said or written before. Neither of us has relied on anything that is not in it. Nothing limits liability for fraud.

16.5 **Variation and waiver.** A change to this agreement is effective only if in writing and signed by both of us. Not enforcing a right is not a waiver of it.

16.6 **Severance.** If any part of this agreement is found unenforceable, the rest continues, and the part in question is changed to the minimum extent needed to make it enforceable.

16.7 **Third parties.** No one other than the parties has any right to enforce this agreement under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999.

16.8 **Counterparts and electronic signature.** This agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.

16.9 **Governing law and jurisdiction.** This agreement and any dispute arising from it, including non-contractual disputes, are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
