# Assignment of Rights and Waiver of Moral Rights

**Internal — Your Business Today Ltd — Version 1.0 — 16 November 2026**

Signed by everyone who writes code, documents or anything else for YBT that may become a Deliverable for a client: directors, employees, contractors and agency staff. It is what lets YBT assign the Foreground IP to a client under clause 9.1 of the Master Services Agreement, and keep the promise about moral rights in clause 9.2.

This deed is made on [date] between **Your Business Today Ltd**, a company registered in England and Wales under company number 17329446, whose registered office is at 8 Eastleigh Close, Sutton, England, SM2 6QY ("**YBT**"), and **[Full name]** of [address] (the "**Contributor**").

## 1. Assignment

1.1 In consideration of £1 and the engagement or employment under which the Contributor works for YBT (receipt and sufficiency of which the Contributor acknowledges), the Contributor assigns to YBT, with full title guarantee and by way of present assignment of present and future rights, all intellectual property rights in everything the Contributor creates, alone or with others, in the course of working for YBT, from the first day of that work, including source code, database schemas, configuration, infrastructure definitions, documents and designs (the "**Works**").

1.2 This includes the Contributor's rights in any Works made with the help of AI tools.

1.3 The Contributor will, at YBT's request and cost, sign any document and do anything else reasonably needed to confirm or register YBT's ownership, including after the work for YBT ends.

## 2. Moral rights

2.1 The Contributor unconditionally and irrevocably waives, in favour of YBT, its licensees and successors in title, and every client to whom YBT assigns or licenses the Works, all moral rights in the Works under Chapter IV of Part I of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, and any similar rights anywhere in the world, so far as the law allows.

## 3. What the Contributor keeps

3.1 This deed does not take from the Contributor anything created before working for YBT or outside that work, nor general skills, know-how and experience.

## 4. Law

4.1 This deed is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
